# Ethnic group by economic activity status, and occupation, England and Wales, Census 2021

Census 2021 data tables showing ethnic groups in England and Wales by economic activity and occupation types. Economic activity includes employment, self-employment, unemployment and reasons for being economically inactive such as being a student, retired, sick, or looking after home or family. These census data are also provided by age and by sex for each subtopic. Ethnic groups described are the 19 tick-box level categories available for Census 2021.
