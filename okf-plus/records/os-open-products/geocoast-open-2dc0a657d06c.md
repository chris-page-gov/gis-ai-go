BGS GeoCoast Open contains a detailed suite of statistical data based on the underlying datasets (GeoCoast Premium). In addition, there is a tool to compare or share best practise at a regional scale and streamline the consideration of multiple underlying datasets through a simple, high-level scheme, presented as domains.; GeoCoast Open consists of: regional statistics (shapefiles); coastal domains for erosion and morphology.
