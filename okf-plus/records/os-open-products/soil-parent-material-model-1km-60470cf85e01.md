# BGS Soil Parent Material Model

Soils are the result of weathering processes that occur on the Earth’s surface where the atmosphere meets the geosphere and hydrosphere. We live in this ‘critical zone’, relying on our soils to grow our food and sustain the biodiversity and health of our environment.; A ‘parent material’ is a soil-science name for a weathered rock or deposit from and within which a soil has formed. In Great Britain, parent materials provide the basic foundations and building blocks of the soil, influencing their texture, structure, drainage and chemistry.; The Soil Parent Material Model details the distribution of physiochemical properties of the weathered and unweathered parent materials of Great Britain to: (i) facilitate spatial mapping of soil properties Great Britain; (ii) identify soils and landscapes sensitive to erosion; (iii) provide a national overview of our soil resource; and (iv) develop a better understanding of weathering properties and processes.
