# Crime in England and Wales: Quarterly Data Tables

Data from Crime Survey for England and Wales (CSEW) and Home Office police recorded crime broken down into quarterly time periods. This workbook gives the estimated number of CSEW crimes experienced in the last 12 months by those interviewed in each quarter between April 2014 and March 2015. The police recorded crime table shows the number of crimes recorded by the police in the particular quarter shown.
