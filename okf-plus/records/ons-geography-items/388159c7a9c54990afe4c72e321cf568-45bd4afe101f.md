# Urban Audit: Functional Urban Areas (December 2011) Generalised Clipped Boundaries in Great Britain

This file contains the digital vector boundaries for the European Urban Audit functional urban areas (UAFUAs) in Great Britain as at 31 December 2011. Urban Audit defines a harmonised geography across Europe for the collection of data about urban areas. It features 160 'core cities' in Great Britain. Of these, 11 have a 'greater city' area, while 38 have a 'functional urban area'. All boundaries are based on local authority districts in England and Wales, or council areas in Scotland. The boundaries are generalised (20m) and clipped to the coastline (Mean High Water mark). Contains both Ordnance Survey and ONS Intellectual Property Rights.
