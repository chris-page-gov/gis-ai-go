# TTWA_2011_UK_BSC

This file contains the digital vector boundaries for travel to work areas (TTWAs) in the UK as at December 2011. This set of TTWAs, built in 2015 from 2011 Lower Layer Super Output Areas (LSOAs) in England and Wales, Data Zones (DZs) in Scotland and Super Output Areas (SOAs) in Northern Ireland and are to be referred to as 2011 TTWAs. These 228 TTWAs covering the whole of the UK were defined using 2011 Census commuting flow data, indicating home and workplace address, and in some cases span country borders. This product contains the nine-character GSS codes only. Super generalised boundaries are designed for national mapping where high levels of detail are not required. They have very small file sizes which also makes them suitable for mapping on the web. They should not be used for GIS analyses. They have also been clipped to the coastline (Mean High Water mark), giving the coastline a more orthodox appearance. Please note that this product contains both Ordnance Survey and ONS Intellectual Property Rights.
