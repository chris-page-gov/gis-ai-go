# ONS Field Recruitment - Pay Band Boundary Query Tool

ONS Field Recruitment - Pay Band Boundary Query Tool This tool will enable you to locate a address or postcode using the search facility and determine whether it's within the 10 mile (Inner London) pay band boundary, the 10 to 18 mile (Outer London) pay band or the 18+ mile (National) pay band. INSTRUCTIONS TO SEARCH FOR A POSTCODE OR ADDRESS: 1. Click on the Search Bar. 2. Type in a postcode or an address and the tool will zoom to the area required. 3. If a postcode is searched for, a pop-up will display the Postcode information. 4. The legend on the right defines the Inner and Outer London and National pay band boundaries from the Charing Cross postcode.
