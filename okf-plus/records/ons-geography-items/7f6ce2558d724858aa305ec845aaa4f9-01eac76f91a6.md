# Regions, England and Wales, 2011

Government Offices for the Regions closed at the end of March 2011. The former Government Office Regions (England) are now referred to as 'regions' for statistical purposes. Wales is not a region, but is often used as an equivalent for the purpose of representing statistics that cover England and Wales. England is made up of the following 9 regions: North East, North West, Yorkshire and The Humber, West Midlands, East Midlands, East of England, London, South East, South West.
