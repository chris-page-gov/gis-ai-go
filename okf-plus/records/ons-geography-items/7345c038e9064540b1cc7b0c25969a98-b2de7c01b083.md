# IPN July 2016

The OS Open Carto map service is designed to be used as background mapping providing a seamless map view from small to large scales with a consistent cartographic representation. The sources of data are Ordnance Survey Strategi data for small and mid-scales and Vector Map District and Open Map Local for the largest scales. The currency of the data is; Strategi - 01/2016 Vector Map District - 03/2016 Open Map Local - 03/2015 The coverage of the map service is GB. The map projection is British National Grid. The service is appropriate for viewing down to a scale of approximately 1:5,000. Basemap last Updated on: 03/06/2016
