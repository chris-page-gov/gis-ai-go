# NHS England (Region, Local Office) to NHS England (Region) (April 2017) Lookup in EN

This file is a lookup between NHS England (Region, Local Office) and NHS England (Region) areas, as at 1 April 2017 in England. Organisation Data Service, part of NHS Digital, received formal confirmation from NHS England that they wished to update the collective organisation role/type names for the NHS Region (Geography) and Commissioning Region areas. The new name for these geography entities are NHS England (Region, Local Office) and NHS England (Region), respectively. This is purely a change to organisation role names. The associated organisation role codes, and the names and codes of individual organisations of this type, will all remain unchanged.
