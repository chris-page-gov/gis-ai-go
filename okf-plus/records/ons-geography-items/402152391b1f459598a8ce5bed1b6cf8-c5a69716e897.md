# NHS Postcode Directory for the UK Full (August 2025)

This file contains the 'Full' NHS Postcode Directory (NHSPD) as at August 2025. The NHSPD relates both current and terminated postcodes in the United Kingdom to a range of current statutory administrative, electoral, health and other geographies. It also links postcodes to pre-2002 health areas and 2001 Census, 2011 Census and 2021 Census Output Areas and Super Output Areas. The NHSPD is issued quarterly. For further technical information about this file, please refer to the User Guide contained within the downloadable zip file.
