# LMCTYUA_2012_EW_BSC

This file contains the digital vector boundaries for inner and outer London, metropolitan counties, counties and unitary authorities in England and Wales as at December 2012. Inner London refers to the part of Greater London falling within the boundaries of the former London county. Outer London refers to the part of Greater London falling outside the boundaries of the former London county. The six metropolitan counties were administrative areas in England from 1974 to 1986, forming the upper tier of a two-tier local government structure. They were abolished in 1986, but are still used for statistical purposes. The non-metropolitan counties form the upper tier of the two-tier local government structure found in many parts of England. Unitary authorities are areas with a single tier of local government. This file contains the nine character coding only for the geographies. For more information, please see the Code History Database (CHD) (link below). Super generalised boundaries are designed for national mapping where high levels of detail are not required. They have very small file sizes which also makes them suitable for mapping on the web. They should not be used for GIS analyses. They have also been clipped to the coastline (Mean High Water mark), giving the coastline a more orthodox appearance. Please note that this product contains both Ordnance Survey and ONS Intellectual Property Rights.
