# National Statistics Postcode Lookup (August 2011) for the UK

This file contains the National Statistics Postcode Lookup (NSPL) for the United Kingdom as at August 2011 in Comma Separated Variable (CSV) and ASCII text (TXT) formats . To download the zip file click the Download button. The NSPL relates both current and terminated postcodes to a range of current statutory geographies via ‘best-fit’ allocation from the 2011 Census Output Areas (national parks and Workplace Zones are exempt from ‘best-fit’ and use ‘exact-fit’ allocations). It supports the production of area based statistics from postcoded data. The NSPL is produced by ONS Geography, who provide geographic support to the Office for National Statistics (ONS) and geographic services used by other organisations. The NSPL is issued quarterly .
