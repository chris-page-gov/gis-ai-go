# NHSPD Online Latest Centroids

This file contains digital vector centroids for the NHS Postcode Directory (NHSPD) as at August 2026. The NHSPD relates both current and terminated postcodes in the United Kingdom to a range of current statutory administrative, electoral, health and other geographies. It also links postcodes to pre-2002 health areas and both 2001 Census and 2011 Census Output Areas and Super Output Areas. The NHSPD is issued quarterly.
