# Rural Urban Classification (2011) of NUTS 3 (2015) in EN

(File Size - 17 KB). The 2011 rural-urban classification (RUC) of 2015 NUTS 3 (Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics) in England is based on the 2011 RUC of Output Areas (OA) published in August 2013, and allows users to create a rural/urban view of local authority district level products. The classification was produced by the University of Sheffield and was sponsored by a cross-Government working group comprising Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, Department of the Communities and Local Government and Office for National Statistics.
