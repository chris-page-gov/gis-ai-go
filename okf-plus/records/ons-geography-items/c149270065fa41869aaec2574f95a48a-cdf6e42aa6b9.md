# lau1

This file contains digital vector boundaries for local administrative units, level 1 in England and Wales as at 1st January 2012. LAU form part of a hierarchical classification of spatial units that provide a breakdown of the EU's territory for producing comparable regional statistics. LAU 1 replaced NUTS 4 in July 2003. They are made up of individual local authority districts. There are 348 LAU 1 areas in England and Wales. Please note that NUTS and LAU do not have the nine character codes as Eurostat use only there own seven character codes. Boundaries (Generalised, Clipped)
