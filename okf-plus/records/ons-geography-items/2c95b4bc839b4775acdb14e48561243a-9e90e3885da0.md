# Broadband coverage across Norfolk

Map Layers Legend Broadband coverage across Norfolk mapped against age, disability and deprivation. This interactive map has been produced for Norfolk County Council by the ONS Local team within the Office for National Statistics. This map uses open data available from OfCom to identify broadband coverage at a postcode level across the County of Norfolk. In addition, the map also includes some demographic data from the 2021 census at an Output Area level. Output Areas (OAs) are the lowest level of geographical area for census statistics, these are made up of between 40 and 250 households and usually have a resident population between 100 and 625 persons. This data includes: disability status as defined by the equality act, residents age, and household deprivation identified from the census deprivation index. ONS Local have produced this map to help Norfolk County Council identify areas where better broadband connectivity is required .
