Updated on 22 October 2026. This lists all 33 families in the current authored investigation, not every vocabulary in the domain. Scheme releases, complete code lists, translations and cross-scheme mappings remain separately bounded. The linked sources support the recorded review scope; a search extract is not a complete page review.

See the [method and findings](CONTROLLED_VOCABULARIES.md), [compact registry](../../../okf-plus/profile/learning-vocabulary.json) and [term discovery method](../../implementation/GEOSPATIAL_TERM_DISCOVERY.md).
