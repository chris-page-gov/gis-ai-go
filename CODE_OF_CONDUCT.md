# Code of conduct

Be respectful, inclusive and specific. Discuss the work rather than the person, make
room for different expertise and accessibility needs, and treat legal, security and
data-governance concerns seriously.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of sensitive material and
bad-faith disruption are not acceptable. Report concerns privately to the repository
owner. The owner may moderate or remove contributions and participation where needed
to protect people or the project.
