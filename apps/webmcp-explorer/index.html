A question becomes a bounded catalogue call

In a supported browser, your AI can choose one of this page's two read-only tools. GIS AI GO then validates the arguments and reads the same governed catalogue that you can inspect here yourself.

No model is embedded in this site. Your question stays with your AI host; this page receives only the structured tool arguments it needs.

The division of labour

Probabilistic interpretation, deterministic data handling

  1. 1 You ask a question Your words stay in the conversation with your AI.
  2. 2 Your AI selects a tool It turns the question into bounded search or record arguments.
  3. 3 The page validates and reads Shared GIS AI GO code searches the checked public catalogue.
  4. 4 You inspect the result Authority, rights, freshness, sources and limits remain visible.

The callable surface

Two page tools, and no hidden third one

explorer_search_catalogue

Search reviewed metadata

Bounded words and governed facets; at most five compact results.

explorer_describe_record

Describe one exact record

Authority, rights, freshness, limitations and linked source records.

Ask this in your AI conversation

Use Site tools. Search the catalogue for ONS statistics, filter to provider, then describe the most relevant record, its sources and its limitations.

In ChatGPT or Codex's supported built-in browser, open the Site tools list, then paste the complete question into the conversation — not into the manual keyword field below. In an ordinary browser, enter ONS statistics below and use the deterministic controls.

Manual fallback and teaching aid

Run the same catalogue logic yourself

This is not an AI prompt box. Enter public-metadata keywords such as ONS statistics. Do not enter a person, address or other personal information.

The architectural lesson

        
WebMCP complements the gateway; it does not replace it

        

          

            
What this page can show

            
    
              
  • Your AI translating a question into narrow structured arguments.
    • 
              
  • Page-scoped discovery without installing a separate MCP server.
    • 
              
  • Deterministic search and source tracing over validated public metadata.
    • 
              
  • The person and AI working from the same visible catalogue.
    • 
              
  • A complete manual fallback when WebMCP is unavailable.
    • 
            

          

          

            
What this page cannot show

            
    
              
  • A service that keeps running after this page closes.
    • 
              
  • Live ONS, HMLR, LandIS or licensed provider execution.
    • 
              
  • MCP 2026-07-28 transport conformance or the exact-five gateway.
    • 
              
  • Durable receipts, later evidence inspection or lost-response recovery.
    • 
              
  • Production identity, authorisation, quotas, registry publication or deployment.
    • 
              
  • That an AI's interpretation or final explanation is correct.
    •
