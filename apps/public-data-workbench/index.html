What does OKF add?

A shared knowledge structure connects source-native dataset identifiers to their concepts and dimensions. These seven frozen records are a small development sample, not the whole ONS catalogue. A match is a candidate for review, not permission to retrieve data.

Concept mappings are authored engineering choices. This search does not use an AI model or claim to understand a full question.

Where are “Site tools”?
They are the page functions a supporting browser exposes to your AI. They are not a menu on this website. Chrome or Edge being up to date does not, by itself, connect an AI to these functions.
