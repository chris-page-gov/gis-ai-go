## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-20

### Added

- Stage 0 repository, research provenance, governance, candidate contracts,
  synthetic fixtures, architecture sources and assurance harness.
- MIT licensing for the project-authored repository and preserved research pack.
- Live context, progress, implementation roadmap and issue-backed delivery model.
- A deterministic, source-locked public OKF bundle with Markdown, JSON, JSON-LD,
  rights boundaries, checksums and reproducible build receipts.
- An accessible static public Explorer with governed catalogue search, facets,
  source-backed graph and timeline views, a non-legal coverage schematic, durable
  URLs, machine-readable downloads, exact-origin browser assurance and symlink-safe,
  checksum-bound static builds.
- Digest-locked HM Land Registry journeys and metadata-only ONS and LandIS
  capability records with explicit source, date, access, rights and non-legal
  boundaries.
- Checksum-bound, provenance-attested GitHub Pages publication with least-privilege
  deployment, live browser assurance and artefact-only rollback.
- Retention of the immutable attested source archive while safely materialising and
  rechecking its exact product bytes for GitHub's pinned official Pages transport.

### Changed

- Superseded the research codename with **GIS AI GO**.
- Authorised evidence-gated public delivery, release management and deployment
  under the GIS AI GO identity.
- Replaced the pre-release source-review instruction with a dated freshness
  statement requiring consumers to recheck official sources for current use.

### Fixed

- Normalised retained Pages source-archive members to an explicit `./` root and a
  deterministic non-root owner, strengthening independent verification before the
  exact publication bytes enter GitHub's supported Pages transport.

### Security

- Added release-level reproducibility, optional-browser-API, visible-focus and
  post-deployment receipt assurance for the public discovery product.
